By phone: Call 1(888)933-1113 for automated or agent-assisted payments — available for urgent needs.

Payment Methods & How-To

Below are short, action-oriented instructions you can follow now.

Guest Payment (no login)

  1. Open the Allstate billing page or call 1(888)933-1113
  2. Choose the guest or one-time payment option
  3. Enter your policy number and required verification
  4. Select payment type and enter payment details
  5. Confirm and save the confirmation number

One-time payment (logged in)

  1. Sign in to your Allstate account
  2. Go to Billing & Payments and select the policy
  3. Enter amount, choose payment method and confirm
  4. Save confirmation and log out when done

Mobile App

Download the Allstate app, sign in, select the policy and tap "Pay Bill." Use saved cards for faster checkout.

By Phone

Call 1(888)933-1113. Follow prompts to make an automated payment or choose to speak with a representative for assistance or confirmation.

Tip: Keep your policy number, ZIP code, and the last 4 digits of your payment card ready to speed up the process.

FAQs

Q: Can I pay without logging in?
A: Yes — use the guest/one-time payment option or call 1(888)933-1113.
Q: How long until my payment posts?
A: Electronic payments usually post quickly; bank transfers may take 1–3 business days depending on the method.
